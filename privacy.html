Privacy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Summary

  • No ads, no tracking, we never sell your data.
  • Your activity records stay on your phone.
  • The community is optional; if you join, only your nickname and what you share are stored on our server.
  • You can permanently delete your account in the app at any time.

Controller and contact

Baby Day Space is provided by an independent developer. For any privacy request: hakkitabak209@gmail.com.

Data that stays on your device

Your baby's name/nickname, date of birth (if entered), activity records (sleep, breastfeeding, bottle, diaper, bath, solids, growth measurements, pumping), notes and app settings are stored only in a local database on your device. They are not sent to us or to any third party. Deleting the app or using Settings › Delete account removes them from the device.

Data processed for the community (optional)

DataPurpose
Random account ID (anonymous session)Link your posts to you, prevent abuse
Nickname, optional baby age range, app languageYour profile and language community
Posts, replies, poll votes, likes, saved postsCommunity service
Your reports, accounts you block, time you accepted the termsModeration and safety
Email address (only if you link your account to email)Sign in to the same profile on another device/web

Photo uploads to the community are not available in this version. Community content is visible to other users; do not share personal details (full name, phone, address). Posts containing phone numbers or email addresses are rejected automatically.

Service provider and location

Community data is stored with Supabase (Supabase Inc.) in the European Union (Ireland, eu-west-1). Supabase processes the data only on our behalf.

Legal basis (GDPR)

Performance of a contract to provide the community; legitimate interest for safety and moderation. On-device records are not processed by us.

Retention and deletion

Community data is kept until you delete your account. Settings › Delete account immediately and permanently deletes your profile, posts, replies, likes, reports and identity. Details: Account deletion.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show a running sleep timer (local notification).

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, object and lodge a complaint (GDPR Art. 15–22, KVKK Art. 11). Requests: hakkitabak209@gmail.com.

Children

Baby Day Space is for parents and caregivers; it is not directed to people under 18 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If we update this policy we change the date above and announce material changes in the app.

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Our standards

Baby Day Space has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

  • Any content that sexualises children is prohibited and removed immediately; the account is permanently terminated.
  • Sharing nude or intimate photos of babies or children is prohibited.
  • Users can report content in the app; reports are reviewed within 24 hours.
  • Identified CSAM is reported to the relevant authorities and, where applicable, to NCMEC.

Account deletion

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Delete in the app (recommended)

  • Open Baby Day Space.
  • Go to Settings › Delete account.
  • Confirm. Deletion is immediate and permanent.

What is deleted

  • Your community profile, nickname, posts, replies, poll votes, likes, saved posts
  • Photos you shared
  • Your reports, block list, terms acceptance record, linked email
  • All activity records and settings on the device

Nothing is retained: no backup or archive is kept after deletion.

If you can't access the app

If you uninstalled the app or cannot open it, email hakkitabak209@gmail.com with the subject "Account deletion" and include your community nickname. We complete the request within 7 days and confirm by email.
